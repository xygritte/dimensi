About Us

DIMENSI Band

Formed in 2018, DIMENSI is a dynamic musical ensemble from UKM Musik UNIDA that has captivated audiences with their unique blend of contemporary and traditional sounds. Our passion for music drives us to create unforgettable performances.

With members specializing in various instruments and vocal styles, we bring versatility and energy to every stage. From intimate acoustic sets to full-band productions, DIMENSI delivers musical experiences that resonate with listeners of all ages.
